REFLECTION

What I learned while building with AI

Building a website with AI was a new and interesting experience for me. AI helped me brainstorm ideas, understand HTML and CSS, organize my page, and troubleshoot problems when something was not working the way I expected.

I also learned that AI is most useful when I stay involved in the process. I still had to test the website, review the code, make changes, and decide what looked right. This experience made me more confident using HTML, CSS, GitHub, and AI together while also teaching me that mistakes and troubleshooting are normal parts of learning web development.
